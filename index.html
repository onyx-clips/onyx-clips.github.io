Features

Everything that comes after the play

Your clips right in the game

Alt+C opens the overlay over the game: your newest clip big, the latest ones next to it. Watch, copy for Discord, Esc takes you back to the game. Onyx injects nothing into the game for this.

Straight into Discord, under your limit

“Copy for Discord”, and Onyx automatically brings the clip under your upload limit: 20 MB without Nitro, 50 MB with Nitro Basic, 1 GB with Nitro. Then just Ctrl+V in Discord.

Vertical for TikTok

Switch on vertical in the editor once, and the landscape clip becomes 9:16. Plus trimming, speed from 0.25× to 2×, your own music and six transitions. Several clips can be combined into one.

Hands full? Just say it

With the voice command, “Clip that” into the mic is enough. Recognition runs only on your PC, offline. You pick the phrase yourself and can try it right away.

Time Travel

Rediscover old clips: one after another, right for Like, left to move on. Like Tinder, just with your best moments.

Your clip year

Every day with clips at a glance, your longest streak and your best day. Right on the home page.

Performance

Weak PC? No problem.

Onyx records with your graphics card's encoder, not with the processor. Onyx picks it by itself: NVENC on NVIDIA, AMF on AMD, Quick Sync on Intel.

Saving re-encodes nothing: the recording waits in short pieces and is only put together. Your game just keeps running.

If it still gets tight, turn down resolution and frame rate, for example to 720p and 30 FPS.

NVIDIA · NVENCAMD · AMFIntel · Quick Sync

No account. No cloud.

Your clips stay on your PC, in the folder Videos\Onyx. The only things that go online are the check for updates and messages and, if you leave them on, an anonymous usage statistic (a random ID, the version, how many clips were saved) and error reports without personal data. Never your clips, names or files.

Questions

Good to know

Does Onyx run on my PC?

Onyx needs Windows 10 (64-bit) or Windows 11. Ideally with a graphics card from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel: then it does the recording and the game hardly notices. Without a suitable graphics card the processor records (x264).

Which games does it work with?

Onyx records your screen and detects the game in the foreground. It doesn't touch the game or load anything into it; the clips land in the folder, sorted by game.

Do I need an account?

No. Install, press F9, done. There is no sign-in and no cloud.

Windows warns me when installing. Is that normal?

Yes. For new programs without a well-known signature Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. Onyx installs only for your user and needs no administrator rights.

How do updates arrive?

Onyx downloads new versions in the background and installs them when no game is running. Every update is digitally signed and checked before it starts. You can switch this off in the settings under “Updates”.

Which languages does Onyx speak?

English, German and Russian. You pick one right at the first start and can change it any time in the settings under “General”.

How do I get rid of Onyx?

Windows Settings → Apps → Onyx → Uninstall. Your clips in the folder Videos\Onyx stay where they are.
